!

Read before quoting “unique visitors”

▾

In all loading… daily rows, Unique visitors = Visits = New Visits — exactly, every single day. No site behaves that way. Matomo here is not recognising returning visitors, so “unique visitors” here is a duplicate of “visits”, not a second measurement. Reporting both to the committee would present one number twice.

This dashboard therefore reports visits and states the limitation. It also means the data cannot currently answer “how many people came back” — the uptake question the committee most likely cares about. Worth raising with whoever administers the Matomo instance before the review.
